03
SEO & GEO

What is GEO, and how is it different from SEO?

People now search two ways: they type into Google, and they ask an AI assistant. Your site has to be found on Google and cited by AI.

Search engine optimization

SEO: rank where people search.

Search engine optimization (SEO) is the work of helping your pages rank higher in Google's results for the searches your customers actually make.

We tune page speed and Core Web Vitals, write titles and descriptions that earn the click, fix crawl and index issues, build internal links, and grow your local presence in Denver and across Colorado through Google Business Profile and reviews. More on our SEO services in Denver.

  • Google Search
  • Google Maps
  • Bing
  • Apple Maps
Generative engine optimization

GEO: be the answer AI gives.

Generative engine optimization (GEO) is the work of making your business easy for AI assistants to understand, trust and cite when they answer a question.

We add structured data that names who you are, where you work and what you offer; write clear, quotable answers to the questions customers ask; keep your facts consistent across the web; and make sure AI crawlers can read your site.

  • Google AI Overviews
  • ChatGPT
  • Gemini
  • Perplexity
  • Claude
  • Copilot
