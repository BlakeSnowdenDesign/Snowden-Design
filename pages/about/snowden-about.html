The story

One lead, full spectrum.

Business strategy and creative production under one roof, from the first sketch to launch day.

Hi, I'm Blake.

I'm the designer and developer behind Snowden Design. I build websites that blend art and technology into interactive, engaging experiences, and I make much of the content that fills them: graphics, digital art, video and drone footage.

I pair the creative work with full-spectrum business development strategy for B2B and B2C clients, backed by a deep technical understanding of the nuances it takes to close sophisticated, high-value deals.

Working this way means the person planning your site is the one designing it, filming it and building it. The idea survives all the way to launch, and you always know who is accountable for it.

What I believe

More than a showcase.

A website should be more than a place to show off products and services. Every plan is built to do three things.

Principle 01

Look good.

Visual design, style guides and motion that make a strong first impression and hold it.

Principle 02

Engage people.

Active digital content, video and interaction that turn a visit into an immersive experience.

Principle 03

Educate them.

Clear website copy and content that help your audience learn, then make the next step easy.
