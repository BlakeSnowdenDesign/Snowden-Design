The story

One studio, full spectrum.

Business strategy and creative production under one roof, from the first sketch to launch day.

Hi, I'm Blake.

I run Snowden Design from Denver, Colorado. I build websites that blend art and technology into interactive, engaging experiences, and I make the content that fills them.

My primary focus is art, mind-blowing interactive visuals, digital and video production, and e-commerce. Around that sits everything a brand needs to launch: full-spectrum business strategy, graphics, illustration, logo design, SEO, social media, content management and drone footage.

Working this way means the person planning your site is the one designing it, filming it and building it. The idea survives all the way to launch.

What I believe

More than a showcase.

A website should be more than a place to show off products and services. Every plan is built to do three things.

Principle 01

Look good.

Visual design, style guides and motion that make a strong first impression and hold it.

Principle 02

Engage people.

Active digital content, video and interaction that turn a visit into an immersive experience.

Principle 03

Educate them.

Clear website copy and content that help your audience learn, then make the next step easy.
